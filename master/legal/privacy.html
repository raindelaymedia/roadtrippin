Privacy Policy

Rain Delay Media Analytics · Last updated October 14, 2026

Rain Delay Media ("RDM", "we") operates an internal analytics tool that measures the performance of the podcasts and creators we work with. This policy explains what information the tool collects from connected social accounts (including TikTok), how we use it, and your choices.

What we collect

Only from accounts whose owners explicitly authorize our app, and only the following:

  • Basic profile information: account ID, display name and avatar.
  • Account statistics: follower count, total likes and number of videos.
  • Public video information and statistics: video ID, title or description, publish date, duration, link, and view, like, comment and share counts.

We do not collect passwords, private messages, contacts, location, payment information, or data about the people who watch or follow an account.

How we use it

To produce internal performance reports and dashboards for the shows we work with and for their sponsors (for example, the views delivered by sponsored content). We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

What we never do

  • Post, edit, delete or message on any connected account.
  • Sell or rent data, or share it with anyone outside the reporting described above.

Storage and retention

Data is stored with the show's analytics records in systems controlled by RDM and kept for as long as we report on that show or partnership. Access tokens are stored securely and are never published.

Your choices

An account owner can disconnect our app at any time in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services), which stops all further collection. To ask us to delete data we hold for your account, email us at the address below.
